---
title: "Set Up PayPal for Subscriptions"
description: "Enroll a PayPal account as a billing agreement (PAYPAL_ENROLLMENT) and use it to create recurring subscriptions."
---

Yuno supports two different PayPal flows. Choosing the wrong one is the most common reason a PayPal subscription is never created.

| Flow | Payment `type` | What you get | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **One-time PayPal** | `PAYPAL` | A single, completed payment. With `vault_on_success: true` it stores a wallet token for *one-click reuse*, **not** a billing agreement. | Single purchases, one-click checkout |
| **PayPal Enrollment** | `PAYPAL_ENROLLMENT` | A **billing agreement / payment-method token** the customer approves once, then reusable for off-session recurring charges. | **Subscriptions and recurring billing** |

<Warning>
**A one-time `PAYPAL` payment cannot back a subscription — even with `vault_on_success: true`.**

To charge a customer's PayPal account on a recurring basis you must enroll it as `PAYPAL_ENROLLMENT` first. The buyer approves the billing agreement on PayPal, and only then can you reference the enrolled payment method in [Create Subscription](/reference/create-subscription). Sending `type: PAYPAL` with subscription intent will succeed as a single charge but will **not** create a reusable subscription token.
</Warning>

## Prerequisites

* A PayPal **connection that supports billing agreements / vaulting** configured on your [Yuno Dashboard](https://dashboard.y.uno).
* `PAYPAL_ENROLLMENT` must be **connected, routed, and added to your checkout builder** for the account — otherwise it will not appear in the methods available to enroll. See [Set up connections](/docs/connections), [Build a route](/docs/routing), and [Configure the checkout builder](/docs/checkout-builder).
* Your [API credentials](/docs/developers-credentials): `public-api-key`, `private-secret-key`, `account_id`.

## Flow overview

```
Create customer ─▶ Create customer session ─▶ Enroll PAYPAL_ENROLLMENT
        ─▶ Redirect buyer to PayPal approval URL ─▶ Buyer approves billing agreement
        ─▶ Confirm status = ENROLLED (get vaulted_token) ─▶ Create subscription
```

## Step 1 — Create (or reuse) the customer

Register the customer with [Create Customer](/reference/create-customer), supplying your `merchant_customer_id`. Reuse the returned `id` if the customer already exists.

## Step 2 — Create a customer session

PayPal enrollment uses the **Checkout workflow**, which requires a customer session. Call [Create Customer Session](/reference/create-customer-session) with the customer `id` and keep the returned `customer_session`.

## Step 3 — (Optional) Confirm PAYPAL_ENROLLMENT is available

Call [Retrieve Payment Methods to Enroll](/reference/retrieve-payment-methods-to-enroll-checkout) with the `customer_session`. `PAYPAL_ENROLLMENT` appears only if it is connected, routed, and in your checkout. If it is missing, revisit the [Prerequisites](#prerequisites).

## Step 4 — Enroll the PayPal account

Call [Enroll Payment Method](/reference/enroll-payment-method-checkout) with `payment_method_type: PAYPAL_ENROLLMENT`. The response contains a redirect URL for buyer approval at `provider.redirect.init_url`.

## Step 5 — Redirect the buyer to approve the billing agreement

Redirect the buyer to `provider.redirect.init_url`. PayPal hosts a billing-agreement approval screen (`paypal.com/agreements/approve`). **The agreement is created only after the buyer approves here** — if the buyer abandons this step, the payment method stays unconfirmed and no reusable token is produced.

## Step 6 — Confirm enrollment

After the buyer returns, call [Retrieve Enrolled Payment Methods](/reference/retrieve-enrolled-payment-methods-api) and confirm the PayPal method has `status: ENROLLED`. Store its `vaulted_token`. We recommend also listening to [Webhooks](/docs/configure-webhooks) for the enrollment status instead of polling.

## Step 7 — Create the subscription

With an `ENROLLED` PayPal payment method, call [Create Subscription](/reference/create-subscription), referencing the customer and the enrolled PayPal payment method (`vaulted_token`). Yuno then charges the billing agreement automatically for each cycle. Listen to subscription webhooks to track each cycle's outcome.

<Note>
**Already collected a one-time PayPal payment?**

A prior `type: PAYPAL` payment (even with `vault_on_success: true`) does not produce a billing agreement. The customer must complete the `PAYPAL_ENROLLMENT` approval in Steps 4–6 before a subscription can be created.
</Note>
